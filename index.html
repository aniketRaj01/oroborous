How it works

Small steps, repeated.

Give a persona a niche

Every account starts with a voice, a topic area and a style, so its videos feel consistent.

Let it make videos

The persona proposes ideas, writes the script and renders a short vertical video with no manual editing.

Publish on a schedule

Videos go out automatically at planned times, aimed at the reach each platform rewards.

Learn and go again

What performed informs the next batch. The output of one round becomes the input of the next.

Vision & roadmap

Where this is going.

Today Oroborous is private and operates a small number of accounts run by its developer. Everything below the first line is a plan, not a product.

  1. Instagram publishing Running

    Automated posting to developer-operated accounts.

  2. YouTube publishing In progress

    Connecting uploads to developer-owned channels via the YouTube Data API.

  3. X publishing Planned

    Not started. No integration exists yet.

  4. A platform for anyone Vision

    The long-term goal is a service where anyone can have their own AI-run account on Instagram, YouTube and X. It is not open, there is no sign-up, and the privacy policy will be updated before any third party's account is connected.
